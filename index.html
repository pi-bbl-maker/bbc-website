Agroecology • Community • Systems

Restoring Soil. Empowering Women. Building Fair Food Systems.

Buffalo Back Collective is an integrated ecosystem dedicated to agroecology, native seed preservation, and women-led agricultural stewardship. We bridge smallholder farmers, women's cooperatives, and urban communities through fair food systems, place-based living labs, and purpose-built technologies.

Explore the Ecosystem Visit Store & Events

Why We Exist

Addressing the systemic failures in our current agricultural and market systems.

Soil & Ecological Degradation

Industrial farming depletes soil health, pollutes local watersheds, and destroys vital native seed biodiversity.

Middlemen Exploitation

Opaque value chains siphon profits away from growers, leaving smallholders economically vulnerable.

Marginalization of Women

Women farmers perform the majority of agricultural labor yet are systematically excluded from land ownership and decision-making.

How We Work

Our four-part methodology aligning ecology, community, and technology.

Agroecology First

Soil health, biodiversity, and native seeds over industrial yields.

Fair Price Models

Transparent pricing for farmer livelihoods and consumer value.

Women-Led Growth

Centering local decision-making and enterprise ownership with women.

Tech with Intent

Digital tools designed to serve human networks and natural ecosystems.

Our 5 Entities

Buffalo Back Consumer Federation (BBCF)

Urban Community, Curation & Market Access

The urban-facing customer arm of the collective. Connects conscious consumers directly with a large network of organic smallholders and native seed savers. Focuses on community building, outreach, and curating native crop varieties through transparent market platforms.

Visit buffaloback.in (eStore) →

16 Doddi Trust

Bio-Regional Living Lab (Ragihalli)

Our place-based living lab in Ragihalli. Functions as the grounded anchor for agroecological interventions, rural circular economies, local education, health, women's leadership, and sustainable community livelihoods.

Learn About 16 Doddi Trust →

Community Enterprises

Grassroots Incubations & Livelihood Units

Independent commercial enterprises nurtured by the collective:

Ragihalli Collective

Farm-to-table bakery and catering canteen serving regional markets and events with clean local produce.

Kai Thota

A community-supported initiative empowering women farmers with collective tools, decentralized production support, and market integration.

Buffalo Back Labs (BBL)

Systems, AgTech & Analytics

The technology and infrastructure engine of the collective. Engineers open platforms, data analytics, and supply chain tools designed to scale agroecological value chains for smallholders and partner networks.

PGSOC

Organic Certification & Quality Assurance

A peer-review certification framework that supports smallholder organic farmers with accessible, community-driven organic verification, guaranteeing ecological integrity without cost-prohibitive third-party barriers.
